**Title**: Dynamic Decoupling and Intra-Sector Dispersion in FTSE 100 Energy

**Description**: This study quantifies the structural integration of the energy sector by measuring the ratio of intra-sector return dispersion to inter-sector covariance over a 62-day horizon. By converting OHLC values to GBP-denominated log returns, we construct two equal-weighted indices—Integrated/Utility and E&P Pure-Play—and calculate their 5-day rolling correlation stability to detect regime shifts in co-movement. We employ a variance ratio test to determine if the energy sector functions as a monolithic block or if E&P assets exhibit idiosyncratic decoupling from the integrated firms. To ensure statistical robustness given the sample size, we utilize the cross-sectional standard deviation of returns within each sub-sector as a proxy for internal liquidity shocks, filtering out volatility outliers associated with identified ex-dividend events. This framework isolates whether systematic risk is driven by sector-wide energy pricing or diverging sub-sector fundamentals, providing a high-fidelity diagnostic of energy market cohesion without requiring external market-capitalization data.
